Our Execution Process

5-Step Turnkey Project Workflow

How we deliver flawless custom ductwork and industrial air handling systems from concept to commissioning.

01

1. Site Visit & Survey

Our site engineers visit your plant to take precise physical measurements, measure CFM & static pressure requirements, and map structural clearance.

02

2. CAD Layout Drafting

We design optimized 2D/3D CAD duct layouts, sizing branch lines and calculation of friction losses according to SMACNA/ACGIH engineering standards.

03

3. Heavy Fabrication

Fabrication at our Sakinaka workshop using CNC laser cutting, plasma welding, and protective anti-corrosion coating (Mild Steel, SS304/316, FRP).

04

4. Site Erection & Fitment

Our experienced erection crew installs main trunking, branch lines, dampers, expansion joints, and support structures with high safety compliance.

05

5. Testing & Balancing

Post-installation pitot-tube velocity profiling, damper regulation, and noise measurement to ensure optimum performance before final sign-off.
