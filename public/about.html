About Hari Engineering Works

Pioneering air moving technology, pollution control equipment, and heavy industrial machinery fabrication since 1989.

Company Background

Over 35 Years of Engineering Trust

Founded in 1989, Hari Engineering Works has grown from a specialized metal fabrication shop into a premier manufacturer of high-pressure centrifugal blowers, axial flow fans, pulse-jet dust collectors, chemical scrubbers, and industrial ducting systems.

We serve core industries including textile mills, pharmaceutical processing plants, chemical refiners, foundries, power plants, and cement works across India and international markets.
